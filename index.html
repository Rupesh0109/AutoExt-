Intelligence at the edge.

Conventional systems rely on a single parameter, often detecting hazards too late or triggering false alarms. We build multi-modal sensor fusion architectures that monitor thermal, optical, and electrical abnormalities simultaneously—aiming to confirm fire risk before catastrophic thermal runaway.

02 // MULTI-MODAL SENSING

The Core Problems We Solve

Current vehicle fire systems often rely on singular parameters like ambient temperature, which leads to severely delayed detection or frequent false alarms. By the time an engine fire or thermal runaway event is confirmed, catastrophic and irreversible damage to the vehicle—and potential danger to occupants—has often already occurred. AutoExt combats this by introducing instantaneous, multi-modal sensor detection to identify and suppress fires at their exact point of origin before they have the chance to escalate.
